Payroll Runs

Payroll/HR prepares the run; Finance & Accounting approves and releases it. Approved and released runs are locked.

Finance & Accounting may return or void a run that has not been released — with a reason — which reopens its period for reprocessing. A Released run needs a reversing adjustment instead.

Employee payslips remain locked until the run is Released; before that, review run-level totals and approval status here.
