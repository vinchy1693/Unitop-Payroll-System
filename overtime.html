Overtime

Managers file overtime requests for their team. The Manager reviews them; once approved, the employee clocks overtime in and out, and the hours are computed when they clock out.

File Overtime Request

Manager only. Pick a team member, the overtime date/window and a reason. The employee cannot file overtime from the portal.

The day type and rate are derived from the date. The Manager must approve before the employee can clock OT in at the kiosk.
